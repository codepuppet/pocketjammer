Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 9, 2026
No account No backend Offline-first

Pocket Jammer is a standalone music app. We designed it to work without creating an account and without requiring you to provide personal information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information.

  • No name, email, phone number, or account information is required to use the app.
  • We do not run our own servers for Pocket Jammer.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Data Stored on Your Device

Pocket Jammer may store app settings locally on your device (for example: tempo, time signature, and other preferences) so the app can remember your choices. This data stays on your device and can be removed by deleting the app.

Audio Export & Sharing

When you export audio, Pocket Jammer writes a WAV file to a temporary location on your device and then opens the iOS share sheet so you can save or share the file using apps you choose (for example: Files, Messages, Mail, or third-party apps).

Any data you share using the iOS share sheet is handled by the destination app or service you select and is subject to their privacy policies.

Analytics, Tracking, and Advertising

Pocket Jammer does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

  • No cross-app tracking.
  • No device fingerprinting.
  • No targeted advertising.

In-App Purchases (Subscriptions)

If Pocket Jammer offers subscriptions or other in-app purchases, purchases are processed by Apple via the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple may provide us with non-identifying information needed to manage entitlements (for example, whether a subscription is active) through StoreKit on your device.

For more information about how Apple handles purchase data, please refer to Apple’s privacy information and App Store policies.

Children’s Privacy

Pocket Jammer is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Security

Since Pocket Jammer does not transmit personal data to our servers, the main security considerations are local to your device. We recommend keeping iOS up to date and using device security features such as a passcode/Face ID.

Your Choices

  • You can delete exported files from the Files app or wherever you save/share them.
  • You can reset app settings within the app (if provided).
  • You can remove all app data by deleting the app.

Changes to This Policy

If Pocket Jammer’s privacy practices change, we will update this page and adjust the effective date above.
